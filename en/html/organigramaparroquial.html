Parish council

It is made up of about twelve members of the community, chaired by the parish priest, who advise him on the running of the parish. Each member represents an area of parish life.

Its decisions are not binding; the council helps the parish move forward in harmony without neglecting any area of activity. It meets every quarter.

Finance council

It meets at the end of each quarter and is made up of three parishioners with some accounting experience. They help the parish priest prepare the annual accounts that must be submitted to the diocesan curia, and advise him on maintenance and improvement works so that they are carried out prudently.

We are here to help

Any questions?
