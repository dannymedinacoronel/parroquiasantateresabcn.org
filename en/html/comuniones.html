Preparing First Communion together

During the school year, children prepare in the parish catechesis to receive Jesus in the Eucharist for the first time, accompanied by their catechists and their families.

  1. RegistrationFill in the form on this page.
  2. We call youThe parish gets in touch with the family.

  4. CelebrationFirst Communion, with family and community.
