Sunday 11:00 Mass and Eucharistic devotions

Of the weekend Masses, the liveliest is the one at 11:00. Every Sunday (except in August) a large gospel choir sings at this Mass.

See times

Other sacraments

Fr Sols hears confessions every Saturday from 18:30 to 19:00 and always shortly before every Mass. Baptisms are held at midday on any Sunday of the year, on request. Confirmation is celebrated every three years.

Parish Caritas and deanery Caritas team

Parish Caritas hands out food to dozens of families one day a month. A deanery-wide Caritas council has been set up to coordinate the work.

First Communion and Confirmation catechesis

Children and teenagers receive regular Christian formation that prepares them for the sacraments of the Eucharist and Confirmation (the latter from the age of 14).

Married couples' group

Learn more →

Married couples' group

Every eight Saturdays, in the evening, a group of six or seven couples studies a papal document with the parish priest. The meeting lasts an hour and ends with a few snacks.

Pastoral care of the sick

On the last Sunday of each month Fr Sols visits elderly people at home, helped by a group of volunteers.

Learn more →

Pastoral care of the sick

On the last Sunday of each month Fr Sols visits elderly people at home if they request it through the contact details on this website. He is helped by a group of volunteers who visit care homes.

Request a visit

Youth group

About every eight Saturdays, a small group of young people, accompanied by a married couple, discusses the Sunday Gospel with the parish priest.

Eucharistic spirituality group

A group of women who meet monthly with a religious sister to deepen their devotion to the Eucharist. Every Wednesday at 19:30 there is half an hour of Benediction and Adoration of the Blessed Sacrament.

Cleaning volunteers

Cleaning volunteers

A special feature of our parish is that a team of people cleans the church one day a month; they have been volunteering for many years.

Parish outings

Each year, whenever possible, there is an Easter outing: a day with Fr Sols where time together and rest help strengthen the parish community.
