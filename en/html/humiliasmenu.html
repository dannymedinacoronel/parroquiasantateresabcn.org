Fr Albert Sols · Eastertide · May 2019

The sublime book of the «Acts of the Apostles»

A New Testament text that the Church sets as the first reading throughout the fifty days of Easter. Saint Luke writes with an ecclesial sense, not so much about the detailed life of each Apostle, but about how the first Christian community lived, the enthusiasm and heroism of the first Christians. He bears witness to their firm faith in the Holy Spirit, who was beginning to act through them in his newborn Church. This book could well be subtitled: «the presence of the Spirit in the first Christians».
