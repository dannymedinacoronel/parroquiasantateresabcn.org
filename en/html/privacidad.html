Privacy Policy

Home/Privacy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

This policy explains how we process the personal data you provide through this website, in accordance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Spanish Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD) and the General Decree of the Spanish Episcopal Conference on personal data protection.

1. Data controller

  • ControllerSanta Teresa de Jesús Parish (Archdiocese of Barcelona)
  • AddressCarrer del Llobregós, 132, 08032 Barcelona
  • Emailalbertsols@gmail.com
  • Data Protection OfficerThe officer appointed by the Archdiocese of Barcelona, via esglesia.barcelona

2. What data we process, why, and on what legal basis

SourceDataPurposeLegal basis
Contact form and emailName, email and your messageTo answer your enquiryYour consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
First Communion registration formDetails of the child and their parent or guardianTo manage registration and catechesisConsent of the parent or guardian (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and Art. 7 LOPDGDD) and legitimate activities of a religious body with its members (Art. 9(2)(d) GDPR)
Support chatName, email and your messagesTo answer your enquiry liveYour consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
Photographs of activitiesImages of the people shownTo share parish lifeConsent of the people concerned or their legal representatives

Data of children under 14 may only be provided by their parents or legal guardians.

3. How long we keep data

Contact and chat data: for as long as needed to answer your enquiry. Catechesis data: for the duration of the course and afterwards for any periods required by applicable civil or canon law. In any case, until you request erasure where applicable.

4. Recipients

We do not disclose data to third parties unless legally required. To provide some services we use processors acting on our behalf: Google (calendar and maps), FormSubmit (emails the contact and First Communion registration forms to the parish; it sets no cookies), Netlify (website hosting) and Tawk.to (chat). Some are based in the United States; transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified or, otherwise, on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

To understand how the forms are used, the website anonymously counts how many times they are viewed, started and sent, and the reason chosen. No names, email addresses, messages or IP addresses are stored, and no cookies are used.

5. Your rights

You may at any time exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability, as well as withdraw your consent, by writing to albertsols@gmail.com or by post to the parish address, stating which right you are exercising.

If you believe we have not handled your request properly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

6. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect data against loss, unauthorised access or alteration.

7. Cookies

See our Cookie policy.
