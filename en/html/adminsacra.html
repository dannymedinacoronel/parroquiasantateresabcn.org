When and how

Celebrating the sacraments

We accompany you at the most important moments of Christian life.

Reconciliation

Fr Sols hears confessions every Saturday from 18:30 to 19:00 and always shortly before every Mass.

Baptism

Baptisms are held at midday on any Sunday of the year, on request.

Confirmation

Confirmation is celebrated every three years. Catechesis prepares teenagers from the age of 14.

Marriage

For weddings and marriage preparation, ask at the parish office on Friday afternoons.
Parish office

Talk to the parish priest

On Friday afternoons and, in fact, shortly before or after every Mass during the week, the parish priest is available for any matter: baptisms, weddings, visits to the sick or any other question.
