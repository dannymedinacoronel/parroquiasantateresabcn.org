When and how

Celebrating the sacraments

We accompany you at the most important moments of Christian life.

Reconciliation

Fr Sols hears confessions every Saturday from 18:30 to 19:00 and always shortly before every Mass.

Ask for another time →

Baptism

Baptisms are held at midday on any Sunday of the year, on request.

Request a baptism →

Confirmation

Confirmation is celebrated every three years. Catechesis prepares teenagers from the age of 14.

Ask for information →

Marriage

Weddings, their preparation and silver, golden or diamond anniversaries: ask at the parish office on Friday afternoons.

Plan a wedding →

Anointing of the Sick

For the sick and the elderly, at home, in hospital or in a care home. If it is urgent, please call the parish.

Request the Anointing →
Wedding anniversaries

Celebrate your silver, golden or diamond wedding

Celebrating years of marriage? At the parish you can give thanks to God for your marriage with a special blessing during Mass, together with your family and the whole community.

Talk to the parish priest

On Friday afternoons and, in fact, shortly before or after every Mass during the week, the parish priest is available for any matter: baptisms, weddings, visits to the sick or any other question.
